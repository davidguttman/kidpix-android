**Undo survives everything now.** Kid Pix keeps a log of what is drawn, so the picture and its whole undo history come back after the app is closed, killed or updated.

- **Nothing is lost when the app is killed.** Every finished stroke, tap or button press is written down the moment it ends. If the tablet kills the app, only a stroke still in progress at that instant is gone (before: up to the last 2 seconds of drawing).
- **Undo after a restart.** Closing and opening the app, or Android stopping it in the background, keeps the undo history. Tap the Undo Guy and the earlier steps come back, one by one.
- **No 30-step limit.** Undo goes back as far as the picture goes, within about 48 MB of stored steps (hundreds of steps for normal drawing; only the oldest steps are dropped, never the picture).
- **Lighter on the tablet.** The app no longer re-saves the whole picture after every change. Background work while drawing is about a quarter of what it was; drawing itself is as fast as in 1.0.0.

The Undo Guy works as before: tap = back one step, double tap = forward, he shakes when there is nothing left, and a new stroke drops the steps you undid.

**Updating from 1.0.0:** the picture on the canvas is kept and becomes the start of the new history. The undo steps made in 1.0.0 are not carried over (1.0.0 kept them only in memory).

**Install / update:** open `kidpix-android-1.1.0.apk` on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
