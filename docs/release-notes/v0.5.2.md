**No more stuck gray strip on Lenovo tablets.** A swipe down from the top of the screen brings Android's bar back over the drawing. Most tablets slide it away again, but Lenovo's leave it up as an empty gray strip with a "..." window button in it. Kid Pix now pushes it away on the next touch, or a moment after the swipe. It also always draws over the whole screen, even where Lenovo reserves a strip.

**Install / update:** download `kidpix-android-0.5.2.apk` below and open it on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
