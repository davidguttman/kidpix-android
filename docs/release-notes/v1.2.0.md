**Saved pictures keep how they were made.** When a picture is saved, Kid Pix now also keeps its undo history. Open it again later and the Undo Guy steps back through how it was drawn, as far as it goes.

- **Pick up where you left off.** Open a saved picture and keep drawing: the picture you saved stays as it was. Save again and you get a second picture next to the first, each with its own history. So one drawing can grow in different directions.
- **Opening a picture never loses the drawing on the screen.** If the drawing on the screen has anything that was not saved, Kid Pix puts it aside first. It shows up as the first picture in the open-a-picture screen, with a small pencil in its corner. Tap it and it comes back with its undo history. These put-aside drawings stay inside the app (they are not added to the tablet's photos); the newest 8 are kept.
- **Undo after opening a picture** now walks back through that picture's own history. (Before, it brought back the drawing that was on the screen; that drawing is now the pencil picture instead.)
- **Saving looks the same.** The picture still goes to Pictures/Kid Pix as a normal image.

**Storage:** the histories use at most about 512 MB on the tablet. If they ever need more, the oldest saved pictures lose their history first (the pictures themselves stay and open as plain pictures), then the oldest put-aside drawings. A picture deleted from the tablet's photos frees its history the next time Kid Pix starts. A picture changed by another app opens as a plain picture.

**Updating from 1.1.0:** the drawing on the screen and its undo history are kept. Pictures saved before this version open as plain pictures, as before.

**Install / update:** open `kidpix-android-1.2.0.apk` on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
