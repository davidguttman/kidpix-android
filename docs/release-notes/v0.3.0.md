Open your old pictures: there's a new folder button right under Save. It shows every picture you've saved as big thumbnails, newest first. Tap one to open it and keep drawing. If you opened the wrong one, the Undo Guy brings back what you had.

Saved pictures now have white paper behind them, so they look right in the Gallery too.

**Install / update:** download `kidpix-android-0.3.0.apk` below and open it on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
