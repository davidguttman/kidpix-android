**Undo goes back further.** Tap the Undo Guy to go back one step, again and again, up to 30 steps. Double-tap him to go forward again. If there's nothing left to undo or redo, he gives a little shake. The pen's second side button works the same way: press once to go back, press twice quickly to go forward.

**You can see which tool you're using.** The selected tool lights up yellow with a red ring, and so do the size, texture or brush you picked for it. Your picks are remembered when you switch tools and come back.

The pencil now starts on the middle size.

**Install / update:** download `kidpix-android-0.4.0.apk` below and open it on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
