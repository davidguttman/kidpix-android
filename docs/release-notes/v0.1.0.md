First release: JS Kid Pix as an Android tablet app with real pen support.

**Install:** download `kidpix-android-0.1.0.apk` below on the tablet, open it, and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. Android 10 or newer.

**Pen**
- Pressure changes line size for the pencil, brushes and eraser
- Hovering the pen previews stamps, letters and the eraser before you touch down
- Hold side button 1, or flip to the eraser end, to erase; your tool comes back when you lift
- Side button 2 is undo
- Palm rejection: a hand resting on the screen doesn't draw while the pen is down or nearby

**Tablet**
- Fullscreen landscape with the drawing filling the screen
- Save puts a PNG in Pictures/Kid Pix
- Your drawing is still there when you come back

Built and tested on a Lenovo TB336FU, whose pen digitizer reports pressure, an eraser end and two side buttons. It should work with any Android active pen: USI, MPP, Wacom, S Pen.
