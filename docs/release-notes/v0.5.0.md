**Easier to see your tool.** The tool you're using now shows inverted, black with a light picture, like old Mac paint programs. The size, ink or brush you picked gets a thick black frame.

**Your tool follows the pen.** While the pen hovers over the page, a little picture of your tool floats next to the tip. Hold the side button (or flip to the eraser end) and it turns into the eraser.

**Undo is instant on tablets.** In 0.4.0, undo could take about four seconds to show up on some tablets. Now it's right away. Double-tap also gives you a little more time.

**Install / update:** download `kidpix-android-0.5.0.apk` below and open it on the tablet. It updates the old version in place.
